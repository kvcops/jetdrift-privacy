Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how JetDrift: Jetpack Arena ("JetDrift", "the game") handles information. In short: we do not collect your personal data.

No accountsNo adsNo analyticsNo purchases

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The game has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and we run no server that stores player data.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (sound, music, controls, chosen skin, map and bot options) and the pilot name you choose are saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Online multiplayer

  • When you play online, your device connects directly to the other players in the same room. To do this, your pilot name and your device's network (IP) address are shared with the players in that room.
  • If you host a public "world room", the room name, player count, map and the host's network address are posted to free public message boards (MQTT brokers) so other players can find the room. The listing is removed when the room closes.
  • There is no text or voice chat. Pilot names are filtered for bad words. Please don't use your real name as your pilot name.

Children

JetDrift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Permissions

The game uses the internet / network permission only for online multiplayer. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer at the email address shown on JetDrift's Google Play store page.
